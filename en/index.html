Criminology · financial crime · economic transparency

Criminal participation and hidden control in the legal economy

I study how criminal actors, sanctioned entities and other sources of financial-crime risk interact with lawful economic systems. My research focuses on companies, ownership structures, market opacity, sanctions and anti-money laundering, combining criminological questions with empirical and computational methods.

04 / About

Research profile

My research sits at the intersection of criminology, economic analysis and financial-crime prevention. I am particularly interested in the point at which formally lawful companies, assets and transactions become vehicles for criminal participation, hidden control or regulatory circumvention.

I combine quantitative and case-based evidence to connect criminological theory with questions that can be examined at company, market and network level. A recurring aim is to make complex economic relationships easier to inspect, compare and translate into practical risk analysis.
