Research profile

Research at the intersection of criminology, economic analysis and financial-crime prevention, with particular focus on criminal participation in lawful markets, hidden control through ownership structures, sanctions enforcement and anti-money laundering. The methodological approach combines structured company and ownership data, network reconstruction, proxy-based screening, case analysis and documentary evidence.

Current research

Research article
Active cooperation for anti-money-laundering purposes in public administrations

Empirical and legal analysis of the duty of active cooperation in public administrations, including how preventive AML obligations are operationalised and translated into the identification and reporting of suspicious activity.

Empirical study

Empirical analysis of company, ownership and case-based signals that may identify criminal infiltration in waste-related economic activities.

Working paper

Research on ownership structures, asset characteristics, market conditions and observable market effects associated with money-laundering vulnerability in real estate.
